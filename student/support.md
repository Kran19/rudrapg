## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. How do I upload my Aadhar and PAN card?**  
You can upload your identification documents directly within the app during the booking or profile completion process. Ensure the images are clear and readable.

**2. Is my personal information secure?**  
Yes, we take data privacy very seriously. All sensitive documents and personal information are securely stored and only used for verification and compliance purposes as detailed in our Privacy Policy.

**3. I am having trouble logging in.**  
Please ensure you are using the correct email/phone number. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to reset it.
